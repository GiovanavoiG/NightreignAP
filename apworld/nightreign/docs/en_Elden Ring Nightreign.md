# Elden Ring: Nightreign

## Where is the options page?

The [player options page for this game](../player-options) contains all the options you need to configure and export a
config file.

## What does randomization do to this game?

Nightreign's *meta progression* is randomized. You start with one Nightfarer and access to the Tricephalos
expedition. The other Nightfarers, expedition access, Shifting Earth events, Remembrance progress and vessels are
items found across the multiworld. Checks are sent the first time you defeat each Nightlord, night boss, field boss
or Shifting Earth boss, when you complete a Remembrance chapter, and when you buy vessels at the Small Jar Bazaar.

Expeditions themselves are unchanged (they are still random 3-day runs). If you are carrying a Remembrance or
Shifting Earth you have not received yet, the game simply does not offer it.

## What is the goal?

Defeat Heolstor the Nightlord (default), all eight base Nightlords, or every Nightlord including the DLC.

## Which items can be in another player's world?

Nightfarers, Expedition Access, Shifting Earth unlocks, Progressive Remembrances, Goblets and Grails, Murk, relics,
Scenic Flatstones and traps.

## When the player receives an item, what happens?

The mod grants it immediately at the Roundtable Hold (relics/Murk/vessels appear in your inventory, Nightfarers become
selectable, expeditions unlock on the map table). Items received mid-expedition are applied when you return.

## Multiplayer

The game must run without Easy Anti-Cheat (the loader does this automatically), so official matchmaking and Everdark
Sovereigns are unavailable. Duo/trio expeditions are possible through Seamless Co-op; only the host's checks count.
